How to play

  1. Press Enable microphone and allow audio access when prompted.
  2. Plug the SpikerBox into your device's audio input and select it from the dropdown.
  3. Press Start the game to calibrate — relax, then contract your muscle when prompted.
  4. Your muscle activation moves the bird up and down. Catch the worms 🐛, avoid the cats 🐱!

The game reads the live audio stream and band-passes it (20–300 Hz) to turn your muscle activation into the bird's height.
