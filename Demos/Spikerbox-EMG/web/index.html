How to play

  1. Choose your control: keyboard (↑ / ↓ arrows) or flip the switch to the SpikerBox microphone and allow audio access.
  2. For the microphone: plug the SpikerBox into your device's audio input and select it from the dropdown above.
  3. Press Start the game. In microphone mode you calibrate first — relax, then contract when prompted; keyboard mode starts right away.
  4. Move the bird up and down to catch the worms 🐛 and avoid the cats 🐱!

In microphone mode the game reads the live audio stream and band-passes it (20–300 Hz) to turn your muscle activation into the bird's height.
